The charter

Four articles. All of it is checkable on Solana.

The launch

Launch from this page. Charter gives your coin its own creator wallet, so its fees never mix with another coin's.

The seats

The first 100 different wallets to buy take a seat, in chain order. The dev who launched it doesn't get one. The seats never change.

The pay

Every hour Charter claims the coin's creator fees and splits 90% evenly between the seats. 10% goes to the seats of $CHARTER.

The rule

A seat only gets paid while its wallet still holds the coin. Sell and your share goes to the seats still holding. Buy back and you're paid again.
